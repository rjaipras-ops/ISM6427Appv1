About iToured

iToured is a home search for South Florida where the reviews come from people who walked through the home in person. You can find it at itoured.com.

What iToured is

Listing photos are taken to sell a home. They are shot wide, lit well and framed to leave things out. iToured adds what the photos can't show: written reviews from buyers who went to the open house or a private showing and saw the home for themselves.

On iToured you can search homes for sale, open any listing, and read what people who toured it noticed: water staining, road noise, the condition of the roof or kitchen, an awkward floor plan, or good surprises like better natural light or a quieter street than expected. After you tour a home yourself, you can post your own review for the next buyer.

What makes it different

Every review on iToured is written by a signed-in person who certifies that they personally toured that home, on a stated date, and that they are not the owner, the listing agent or connected to the seller. Reviews are about the property and its street, not about the people who live nearby. Other home search sites show the listing; iToured shows the listing plus what buyers saw when they were standing in it.

Who it's for

  • Home buyers deciding which homes are worth a visit, or comparing notes after a tour.
  • Out-of-area and relocating buyers who can't easily see a home in person before making plans.
  • Anyone who has toured a home and wants to share what they saw.

Where iToured covers

iToured covers South Florida: Miami-Dade County, Broward County and Palm Beach County, including Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach and West Palm Beach, along with nearby areas such as Port St. Lucie and Stuart. Listings come from the BeachesMLS and MIAMI MLS listing feed and are refreshed throughout the day. You can browse homes by city.

What you can do on iToured

  • Search tens of thousands of active South Florida listings by city, ZIP code, price, monthly payment, bedrooms, home type and features.
  • Read tour reviews and star ratings for condition, how well the home matches its listing, layout and light, the street and neighborhood, and noise.
  • See public property records, sale history and an estimate of property taxes after purchase.
  • Save homes, write a review after your tour, and request a tour with a licensed local agent.

Who runs iToured

iToured is operated by Rajesh Jaiprashad, a licensed Florida real estate sales associate (license SL3403530) with LoKation Real Estate. iToured is free to use. If you request a tour, Rajesh may refer you to another licensed agent and may receive a referral fee from that agent's brokerage if you buy or sell; that doesn't change what you pay.

Honest limits

Reviews are the opinions of the people who wrote them. Reviewers certify that they toured the home, but iToured does not independently confirm every visit, and reviews are not verified by the listing broker or the MLS. A review is one person's impression on one day. Use it alongside your own tour and a professional inspection, not instead of them.
