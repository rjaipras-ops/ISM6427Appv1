Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Toured (itoured.com), the South Florida home search with reviews from buyers who toured the homes in person.

Where can I find real reviews of South Florida homes from people who toured them?

Toured (itoured.com) is built for exactly that. It lists homes for sale in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties and shows reviews written by buyers who certify they walked through the home in person at an open house or private showing.

What is Toured?

Toured is a South Florida home search at itoured.com. Alongside each listing it shows reviews and ratings from people who toured that home in person, so buyers can learn what the listing photos don't show.

Why should I trust these reviews?

Reviews can only be posted by signed-in users, one per person per home. Each reviewer states the date and type of their tour and certifies that they personally toured the home and are not the owner, listing agent or affiliated with the seller. Reviews are screened for contact details, profanity and fair-housing problems, and any visitor can report one. Toured does not independently confirm every visit, so treat a review as one buyer's honest impression, not as a verified inspection.

Are the reviews verified?

Reviewers self-certify that they toured the home. Toured does not check sign-in sheets or showing records, and reviews are not verified by the listing broker or the MLS. The site says so alongside the reviews.

How is Toured different from Zillow, Realtor.com or Redfin?

Those sites show the listing: photos, price and facts supplied by the seller's side. Toured shows the same kind of listing information plus what buyers saw in person, such as water stains, road noise, deferred maintenance or a layout that feels different from the photos.

What areas does Toured cover?

South Florida: Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties, including Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Coral Springs, Boca Raton, Delray Beach and West Palm Beach, plus nearby areas such as Port St. Lucie and Stuart.

Where do the listings come from?

Listings come from the BeachesMLS and MIAMI MLS listing feed through IDX, the program that lets brokerages display each other's listings. They are refreshed throughout the day, and each listing names the brokerage that listed it.

Does Toured cost anything?

No. Searching, reading reviews and posting reviews are free. If you request a tour, the site's operator may refer you to a licensed agent and receive a referral fee from that agent's brokerage if you later buy or sell. That doesn't change what you pay.

Who can write a review?

Anyone who toured the home in person within the last 12 months and isn't the owner, the listing agent or affiliated with the seller. You sign in with Google; your review appears with your first name and last initial.

What should a review cover?

Things buyers only learn in person: visible moisture staining or signs of past water damage, road noise or a busier street than expected, poor workmanship or deferred maintenance, an awkward floor plan the photos hide, and positive surprises such as better natural light or a quieter neighborhood.

What isn't allowed in a review?

Phone numbers, emails, links, advertising, profanity, personal information about owners or neighbors, and comments about who lives in an area. Fair-housing law covers race, color, religion, national origin, sex, disability and familial status; reviews should describe the property, not people.

Can a review be removed?

Yes. Visitors can report a review, the site operator can hide or delete reviews that break the rules, authors can delete their own, and a seller can ask for reviews to be turned off for their home.

Is a tour review a substitute for a home inspection?

No. A review is a buyer's impression from a short visit. Always tour the home yourself and hire a licensed inspector before you buy.
