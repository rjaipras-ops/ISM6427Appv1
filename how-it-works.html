How iToured works

iToured (itoured.com) pairs South Florida home listings with reviews from people who toured those homes in person. Here is how it works for readers and for reviewers.

If you're looking at homes

  1. Search. Look up a city, ZIP code, neighborhood or address in Miami-Dade, Broward or Palm Beach County. Filter by price, monthly payment, bedrooms, home type and features such as a pool or waterfront.
  2. Open a home. Each listing shows photos, facts, public records, sale history and a tax estimate. If someone has toured it, their reviews and ratings appear on the same page.
  3. Read what tours found. Reviews describe what the listing doesn't: condition, smells, noise, light, layout and the street.
  4. See it yourself. Request a tour and a licensed local agent will contact you.

If you've toured a home

  1. Sign in with Google. Reviews can't be posted anonymously. Your review shows your first name and last initial; your email is never shown.
  2. Find the home and choose "Write a tour review".
  3. Say when and how you toured: open house, private showing or builder tour. The tour must be within the last 12 months.
  4. Rate and describe it. Give star ratings and write, in your own words, what you saw in person.
  5. Certify it. You confirm that you personally toured the home on that date and that you aren't the owner, the listing agent or affiliated with the seller.

What makes a useful review

  • Visible moisture staining, or signs of previous water damage.
  • Road noise, or a busier street than you expected.
  • Poor workmanship or signs of deferred maintenance.
  • An awkward floor plan that the photos hide.
  • Positive surprises, like better natural light or a quieter neighborhood than expected.

Describe the home and the street. Don't describe the people who live nearby.

How reviews are kept honest

  • One review per person per home, tied to a signed-in account.
  • Every reviewer certifies an in-person tour and that they have no connection to the seller.
  • Reviews containing contact details, links, profanity or wording that raises fair-housing concerns are held for a check before they appear.
  • Any signed-in visitor can report a review. Reviews that draw several reports are hidden until they are checked.
  • The site operator can remove reviews that break the rules, and a seller can ask for reviews to be turned off for their home.

iToured does not independently confirm each visit, and reviews are opinions, not inspections. See the FAQ for more, or read about iToured.
