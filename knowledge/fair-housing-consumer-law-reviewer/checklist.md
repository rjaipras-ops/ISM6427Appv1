# Checklist

## Fair housing
- No search, filter, ranking, wording or AI answer about who lives in an area or how safe it is
  (race, color, religion, national origin, sex, disability, familial status, age of neighbors,
  crime, "safe", "good neighborhood").
- AI search marks such requests "declined" and still applies the rest; keep it that way.
- Review screening holds common fair-housing wording for the owner's check.
- No crime or demographic overlays next to listings.

## Contact consent (calls, texts, email)
- Checkbox, unchecked by default, separate from other terms.
- Names who may contact the person (LoKation Real Estate, or a licensed agent it refers them to).
- "Consent isn't required to buy anything", message and data rates, "reply STOP".
- The exact text shown is recorded with each request (consent_text, consent_version, language).
- Spanish consent must say the same thing; changes in either language need review in both.
- Referral networks that contact the person themselves (for example Agent Pronto) need consent
  that covers them. Owner decision pending: name the service in the checkbox, or name it in the
  privacy policy only.

## Disclosures
- Referral fee: "If you're referred to another agent, LoKation may receive a referral fee. It
  doesn't change what you pay."
- Florida real estate advertising: the brokerage name appears in ads and on the site.
- Privacy policy lists what is shared with referral agents and networks.

Open items go to `open-questions.md`.
