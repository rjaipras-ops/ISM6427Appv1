# Voice and flows

- Plain words, short sentences; say the limit next to the feature.
- Buyers mostly use phones; key actions reachable with one thumb.
- Tour request: name, email, phone, timeline, financing, "already have an agent?", message,
  consent. People with an agent are told to contact their own agent and aren't sent through.
- Reviews: free-form, with idea prompts; reviewers certify an in-person tour.
- Never imply a review or estimate is verified by the MLS or the listing broker.
