Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Toured (toured.netlify.app) is operated by Rajesh Jaiprashad, a licensed Florida real estate sales associate (license SL3403530) with LoKation Real Estate. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password, and we don't request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Tour reviews. Reviews you post (ratings, text, tour date and type, and your certification that you toured the home) are published on the site with your first name and last initial.
  • Tour requests. If you send a tour request, we receive the name, email, phone number and message you enter, and the home you asked about.
  • Browser storage. Saved homes and some preferences are kept in your own browser. A secure cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Basic server logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, show your reviews, and stop duplicate or abusive posts.
  • To respond to tour requests and help you buy a home. With your consent on the request form, we may contact you by email, phone or text. We may connect you with another licensed real estate professional to assist you, as permitted by Florida law.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never show your email address publicly.

Who else processes data

  • Google: sign-in (Google Identity Services).
  • Netlify: website hosting, data storage and tour-request form handling.
  • Map and data providers (Esri, OpenStreetMap, U.S. Census geocoder, Florida Department of Revenue, county appraiser and tax sites), which receive map views or addresses you look up, not your identity.
  • BeachesMLS / FBS (Flexmls): provides the listing data and the MLS Search page.

Your choices

  • You can delete any review you posted from the review itself.
  • You can sign out at any time, and you can remove Toured's access in your Google Account under Third-party connections.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your account information, send a request through the contact form and write "Privacy request".

Children

The site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted on the site.
